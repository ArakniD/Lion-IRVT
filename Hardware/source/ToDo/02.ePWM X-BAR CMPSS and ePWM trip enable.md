## Update the ePWM TRIP Zones by EN_ and MODE_ configuration

ePWM X-BAR, CMPSS and ePWM module allocation are currently compiled out. These need to be enabled and set to be the near maximum and minimum current values on the board, such that when Icurrent_Sense from the C2000 pathway can trip the slot, the sense IoutS1-8 from the schematic drvie via an instrumental amplifier with a common mode voltage of 1.25V, meaning the 1.25V is "zero" current, where 0 voltage equals -10A aka a discharge current, and +2.5V is a +10A charge current. The range of the C2000 ADC is 0-3.0V via the onboad REF5030 with fet op-amp buffers.

These trip values are for board protection and must honor the boade EN_[A-C] where slots can be enabled/disable and Grouped via the MODE_[A-C].

Trip zones when slots are all individual drive trip to only their single slot.

Trip zones when slots are in groups of two must trip the group of slots, meaning when paired, trip both slots in pairs such as:

Slot grouping is performed by MODE_[A-C] on GPIO 54-56

Ungrouped : MODE_[A-C] = 0b111

Grouping of 2 : MODE_[A-C] = 0b110
-- CMPSS1 or CMPSS2 trips ePWM1 and ePWM2
-- CMPSS3 or CMPSS4 trips ePWM3 and ePWM4
-- CMPSS5 or CMPSS6 trips ePWM5 and ePWM6
-- CMPSS7 or CMPSS8 trips ePWM7 and ePWM8

Groups of 4 : MODE_[A-C] = 0b101
-- CMPSS1 or CMPSS2 or CMPSS3 or CMPSS4 trips ePWM1 and ePWM2 and ePWM3 and ePWM4
-- CMPSS5 or CMPSS6 or CMPSS7 or CMPSS8 trips ePWM5 and ePWM6 and ePWM7 and ePWM8

Grouping of 8 : MODE_[A-C] = 0b100
-- CMPSS[1-8] trips ePWM[1-8]

Slot enablement is performed by EN_[A-C] on GPIO 57-59

EN_[A-C] = 0b111
-- Slot 1-8 enabled

EN_[A-C] = 0b110
-- Slot 1-7 enabled

EN_[A-C] = 0b101
-- Slot 1-6 enabled

EN_[A-C] = 0b100
-- Slot 1-5 enabled

EN_[A-C] = 0b011
-- Slot 1-4 enabled

EN_[A-C] = 0b010
-- Slot 1-3 enabled

EN_[A-C] = 0b001
-- Slot 1-2 enabled

EN_[A-C] = 0b000
-- Slot 1 enabled only

Disabling a slot will disable a grouping of slots also, meaning if slot 8 is disable, the grouping of 7-8, 5-8 and 1-8 is disabled also.

The grouping and enablement of slots is performed on power-on only.

Enablement and grouping needs to be reported in the unit stats, on both the BTLE and WiFi APIs.

The ESP32 when accumulating current and wattage, must sum the individual slot accumulators for the group configuration as they are still to be calculated on a individual slot basis.

The F28379d CPU1 must calculate wattage by using the group-master slots voltage, while maintaining individual slot current for current accumulation, because when in group mode, the lead ADS131M08 voltage for each group will be connected to the battery under test as a 4-wire measurement, where each slave slots ADS131M08 pathway will be connected at the slots exit/entry point to allow the reverse polarity protection circuit to function, thus this voltage will have an offset vs the real cell voltage under test.

The group master slot is the lowest slot number of each group. such that in groupings of 2, slot 1,3,5,7 become group masters for group 1,2,3 and 4. in groupings of 4, slot 1 and 5 become group masters of group 1 and 2. and in grouping of 8, slot 1 is the master of group 1.

The ePWMs must be out-of-phase when in groups to reduce current noise when similtanious switching. such that groups of 2 are 180 degree oout-of-phase, groups of 4 are 90 degrees out of phase, and grouping of 8 are 45 degree out of phase. Using the table below will ensure that the default phase difference between each slot, when combined into groups shall always obey the 180,90,45 degree rules and ensure that when all slots are switching independantly, that they are out-of-phase by design

| Slot | Phase degree |
|---|---|
| 1 | 0 |
| 2 | 180 |
| 3 | 90 |
| 4 | 270 |
| 5 | 45 |
| 6 | 225 |
| 7 | 135 |
| 8 | 315 |
