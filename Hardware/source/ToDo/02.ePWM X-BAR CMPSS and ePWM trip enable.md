## Update the ePWM TRIP Zones by EN_ and MODE_ configuration

ePWM X-BAR, CMPSS and ePWM module allocation are currently compiled out. These need to be enabled and set to be the near maximum and minimum current values on the board, such that when Icurrent_Sense from the C2000 pathway can trip the slot, the sense IoutS1-8 from the schematic drvie via an instrumental amplifier with a common mode voltage of 1.25V, meaning the 1.25V is "zero" current, where 0 voltage equals -10A aka a discharge current, and +2.5V is a +10A charge current. The range of the C2000 ADC is 0-3.0V via the onboad REF5030 with fet op-amp buffers.

These trip values are for board protection and must honor the boade EN_[A-C] where slots can be enabled/disable and Grouped via the MODE_[A-C].

Trip zones when slots are all individual drive trip to only their single slot.

Trip zones when slots are in groups of two must trip the group of slots, meaning when paired, trip both slots in pairs such as:

Slot grouping is performed by MODE_[A-C] on GPIO 54-56

Ungrouped : MODE_[A-C] = 0b111

Grouping of 2 : MODE_[A-C] = 0b110
-- CMPSS1 or CMPSS2 trips ePWM1 and ePWM2
-- CMPSS3 or CMPSS4 trips ePWM3 and ePWM4
-- CMPSS5 or CMPSS6 trips ePWM5 and ePWM6
-- CMPSS7 or CMPSS8 trips ePWM7 and ePWM8

Groups of 4 : MODE_[A-C] = 0b101
-- CMPSS1 or CMPSS2 or CMPSS3 or CMPSS4 trips ePWM1 and ePWM2 and ePWM3 and ePWM4
-- CMPSS5 or CMPSS6 or CMPSS7 or CMPSS8 trips ePWM5 and ePWM6 and ePWM7 and ePWM8

Grouping of 8 : MODE_[A-C] = 0b100
-- CMPSS[1-8] trips ePWM[1-8]

Slot enablement is performed by EN_[A-C] on GPIO 57-59

EN_[A-C] = 0b111
-- Slot 1-8 enabled

EN_[A-C] = 0b110
-- Slot 1-7 enabled

EN_[A-C] = 0b101
-- Slot 1-6 enabled

EN_[A-C] = 0b100
-- Slot 1-5 enabled

EN_[A-C] = 0b011
-- Slot 1-4 enabled

EN_[A-C] = 0b010
-- Slot 1-3 enabled

EN_[A-C] = 0b001
-- Slot 1-2 enabled

EN_[A-C] = 0b000
-- Slot 1 enabled only

Disabling a slot will disable a grouping of slots also, meaning if slot 8 is disable, the grouping of 7-8, 5-8 and 1-8 is disabled also.

The grouping and enablement of slots is performed on power-on only.

Enablement and grouping needs to be reported in the unit stats, on both the BTLE and WiFi APIs.

The ESP32 when accumulating current and wattage, must sum the individual slot accumulators for the group configuration as they are still to be calculated on a individual slot basis.

The F28379d CPU1 must calculate wattage by using the group-master slots voltage, while maintaining individual slot current for current accumulation, because when in group mode, the lead ADS131M08 voltage for each group will be connected to the battery under test as a 4-wire measurement, where each slave slots ADS131M08 pathway will be connected at the slots exit/entry point to allow the reverse polarity protection circuit to function, thus this voltage will have an offset vs the real cell voltage under test.

The group master slot is the lowest slot number of each group. such that in groupings of 2, slot 1,3,5,7 become group masters for group 1,2,3 and 4. in groupings of 4, slot 1 and 5 become group masters of group 1 and 2. and in grouping of 8, slot 1 is the master of group 1.

The ePWMs must be out-of-phase when in groups to reduce current noise when similtanious switching. such that groups of 2 are 180 degree oout-of-phase, groups of 4 are 90 degrees out of phase, and grouping of 8 are 45 degree out of phase. Using the table below will ensure that the default phase difference between each slot, when combined into groups shall always obey the 180,90,45 degree rules and ensure that when all slots are switching independantly, that they are out-of-phase by design

| Slot | Phase degree |
|---|---|
| 1 | 0 |
| 2 | 180 |
| 3 | 90 |
| 4 | 270 |
| 5 | 45 |
| 6 | 225 |
| 7 | 135 |
| 8 | 315 |

> **Correction, 2026-10-09.** The groups above name CMPSS*n* for slot *n*.
> On this board that holds for slots 1, 4 and 6 only: each comparator's
> input pins are fixed by the device, and slots 2, 3, 5, 7 and 8 have their
> current-sense nets on CMPSS3, 2, 7, 8 and 5. The firmware now binds each
> slot to the comparator on its own current pin, so a pair is slot 1's
> comparator OR slot 2's (CMPSS1 | CMPSS3), and so on. Quads and the octet
> were unaffected. Table: `Docs/hardware-resources.md` section 5.0.

Implemented as specified, plus one defect found on the way in. Changelog below.

### The defect this uncovered

**The CMPSS trip thresholds were computed against the wrong zero, and the
high-side trip was unreachable.**

`bts_user_settings.h` assumed 0 A sat at DAC mid-scale (2048). The sense chain
- as the same file already documented at `BTS_F28I_GAIN_DEFAULT`, measured on
hardware 2026-09-21 - puts 0 A at the instrumentation amplifier's **1.25 V**
common mode. Against the measured 3.019 V reference:

| | firmware computed | sense chain actually needs |
|---|---|---|
| +8 A | 3686 counts = 2.718 V = **+11.7 A**, past full scale | 3051 |
| -8 A | 410 counts = 0.302 V = **-7.6 A** | 339 |

So the positive trip could never fire and the negative one fired 5% early. It
was invisible because every trip was disabled: the thresholds were computed,
written to the DAC, and never consulted.

### Decisions taken where the spec left room

- **Trip level +/-9.5 A**, above the software trip's 8 A rather than equal to
  it. The two are layered: software catches an ordinary over-current within a
  control period, the comparator catches a short within a switching cycle.
  Equal levels would let the hardware win every race and leave the software
  path, and its diagnostics, untested. 0.5 A of headroom remains below the
  sense chain's 10 A full scale.
- **Enabled with a boot-time arm delay.** `BTS_HAL_setupTripRouting()`
  configures everything and arms nothing; `serviceTripArming()` arms on first
  run and disarms when the last slot stops. An unpowered sense chain outputs
  ~0 V, which on a 1.25 V-centred chain reads as -10 A - arming at boot would
  latch a one-shot trip on every slot before the board did anything, and a
  naive clear just re-latches. Nothing is lost: an unarmed trip only matters
  while current is flowing.
- **A partial group is left entirely unrouted.** The spec says disabling slot
  8 also disables the groupings that contained it; this is implemented as
  all-or-nothing per group rather than dropping only the masked slot. A group
  missing a member cannot safely run, so arming its survivors would suggest it
  was usable.
- **The phase table is a 3-bit reversal of the slot index.** The table in the
  spec is exactly that, and the property is what makes it work: every aligned
  power-of-two block of a bit-reversed sequence is itself evenly spread, so
  one table satisfies the 180/90/45-degree rules simultaneously and needs no
  recomputation per grouping.
- **Ungrouped slots are now phased too.** Mode 0 previously left every slot
  free-running and nominally in phase - the case where eight legs can switch
  on the same edge. They are now spread 45 degrees apart by design.

### What had to change that the spec did not mention

- **The comparators cannot reach a trip zone through `OSHT1`.** TZ1/TZ2 are
  hardwired to Input X-BAR INPUT1/INPUT2, not to the ePWM X-BAR, and both sat
  at their GPIO0 reset default - which this board muxes as EPWM1A, so channel
  1's trip zone was watching its own gate drive. The routing now goes through
  the **Digital Compare** submodule (`DCAEVT1`, also a one-shot), which is the
  path `TRIP4..TRIP12` actually feed. `INPUT1`/`INPUT2` are left alone.
- **`DCAEVT1` has its own action register** and does not follow TZA/TZB, so
  the trip action had to be set for it explicitly or the flag would latch
  without the pins moving.
- **`epwmTripISR()` had to learn the new flag.** It tested only
  `EPWM_TZ_FLAG_OST` and would have seen nothing, forever.
- **`BTS_tripEpwm()`'s clear had to be scoped.** It clears the OST flag every
  control pass; left alone it would also have cleared `DCAEVT1`, releasing a
  genuine hardware over-current within a control period of it latching and
  defeating the point of the comparator.
- **Group current was being copied to followers undivided.** A group of eight
  set to 5 A would have drawn 40 A. Now divided by the count of enabled
  members, in one place, with followers copying the already-divided share.

### Before running this on a board

The trips are armed on first run. If the current-sense chain does not behave
as documented, or the comparator inputs are not wired as the pin map claims,
**every slot will latch an over-current the moment it starts** rather than
failing quietly. Have a slot's PWM on a scope for the first run, and start at
a low current.
